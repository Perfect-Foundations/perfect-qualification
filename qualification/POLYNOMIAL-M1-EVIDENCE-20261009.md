## M1 source disposition and blockers

The PR #10 modular specialization retains major favorable-case speedups, but new independently constructed **wide shared-factor** cases repeatably regress against PR #8 (complete Z[x] GCD ~3.28→7.50 µs; Q[x] ~6.16→9.17 µs). A generalized low-degree-difference GCD replacement was mathematically correct but caused severe unlucky-prime slowdowns and is rejected. Negative source/benchmark evidence is retained in the separate Polynomial adversarial branch.

Consequently **PR #8 is the conservative initial Polynomial M1 integrated source baseline**; PR #10 remains experimental pending a robust improvement or explicit qualified dispatch restrictions. This does not mean PR #8 itself is fully qualified or release-ready.
